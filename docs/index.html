Questions

Does Cuisine.AI replace my till (POS)?+

No. Your till keeps taking payments; Cuisine.AI handles food safety, purchases, stock and costs.

Will my kitchen team manage to use it?+

Yes. Staff pick their name on the tablet and tap. Temperatures can even be said out loud.

What if the AI misreads an invoice?+

Nothing is saved until someone checks it, and uncertain lines are highlighted.

Is HACCP extra?+

No. Food safety and HACCP are included in every plan.
