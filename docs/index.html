You have questions? We have answers

Does Cuisine.AI replace my till (POS)?+

No. Cuisine.AI handles food safety, purchases, stock and costs; your till keeps taking payments.

How much does Cuisine.AI cost and how can I try it?+

Plans start at 80 € per month per site. Every plan starts with a 14-day free trial.

Will my kitchen team manage to use it?+

Yes: staff pick their name on the tablet and tap. Temperatures can even be said out loud.

What if the AI misreads an invoice?+

Nothing is saved before someone confirms, and uncertain lines are highlighted.

How does Cuisine.AI help me increase my margin?+

Purchase prices are read from every invoice, so dish costs stay right, price rises are flagged and dishes above your target are pointed out with a suggested price.
