Pricing questions

What counts as an invoice scan?+

One supplier document read by the AI. A five-page invoice counts once; a PDF holding three invoices counts as three. Entering an invoice by hand is always free and unlimited.

What happens if I use all my scans?+

Nothing stops working. You can keep entering invoices by hand, add a pack of 100 scans, or move up a plan at any time.

Are prices per restaurant?+

Yes. Prices are per site, per month, excluding VAT. Every plan includes your whole team.

Can I change plan or cancel?+

Yes. Move up or down whenever you like. Monthly plans can be stopped at the end of any month; annual plans are paid for the year and cost less.

Is food safety really included?+

Yes, in every plan. Temperatures, checklists, traceability, oil and the inspection report are part of Cuisine.AI, not an extra.

Is there a setup fee?+

No. Create your account and start; on the Business plan we also help you set everything up.
